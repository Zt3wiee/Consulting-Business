FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas do you advise on?

We work across three areas: IT consulting, finance and business consulting, and commodities and trading consulting. Because we cover all three, we can look at how a decision affects your technology, your finances and your commercial position together.

What kinds of organisations do you work with?

Technology companies, financial services and fintech businesses, trading companies and commodity merchants, importers and exporters, startups and SMEs, and corporate groups. We tailor each engagement to the client’s industry, operating model and objectives.

How does an engagement work?

We follow four steps: discover and understand your objectives, analyse and evaluate the options, develop a strategy with clear recommendations, and support implementation as agreed with you.

Is every service available for every client?

Specific services are subject to the company’s qualifications, applicable licensing requirements, and the scope agreed with each client.

Can you help with more than one area at the same time?

Yes. Many business decisions involve technology, finance and commercial strategy at once, and we can bring these perspectives together in a single engagement.

How do we get started?

Send us a business inquiry describing your objectives and the area you need help with. Our team will then arrange an initial consultation.

Have more questions?

Send us a message – we are happy to answer any question about how we can help your business.
