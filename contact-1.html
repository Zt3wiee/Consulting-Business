FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

We work with start-ups, small and medium-sized businesses, family businesses and established companies. Whether you are just getting started or planning your next stage of growth, we tailor our advice to your situation.

How does the first consultation work?

The first meeting is free. We listen to your goals and challenges, ask questions about how your business runs today, and suggest the best next steps – with no obligation to continue.

How much do your services cost?

Every business is different, so we do not use fixed price lists. After the first consultation we send a clear quote based on the scope of work, so you know the cost before anything starts.

Can you help foreign investors set up a company?

Yes. We guide investors through company registration, licensing and the practical steps of starting to operate, and we can connect you with trusted local partners where needed.

How long does a typical project take?

A one-time consultation can be completed within days. Larger projects such as a business plan, company setup or restructuring usually take a few weeks to a few months, depending on the scope.

Will my business information be kept confidential?

Absolutely. Everything you share with us is treated as strictly confidential and is only used to give you the best possible advice.

Have more questions?

Call us or send a message – we are happy to answer any question about how we can help your business.
