About Inner Circle

We help businesses grow with clear, practical advice.

Our Story

Inner Circle Limited was founded with a simple belief: every business owner deserves the kind of trusted, experienced advice that large corporations take for granted. Too many good businesses struggle not because of a bad idea, but because they lack the right guidance at the right time.

Our name reflects how we work. We aim to become part of your inner circle – the small group of people you trust with important decisions. We listen carefully, speak honestly, and stay with you from the first conversation until the results are real.

Who We Are

A consulting partner that works as part of your team.

We combine business, finance and operational experience to help owners and management teams make better decisions. Our advice is practical, based on your real numbers, and designed to be put into action.

We value honesty, confidentiality and long-term relationships. Many of our clients start with a single question and stay with us as their business grows.

Common Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Can’t find what you are looking for? Contact us and we will be happy to help.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

We work with start-ups, small and medium-sized businesses, family businesses and established companies. Whether you are just getting started or planning your next stage of growth, we tailor our advice to your situation.

How does the first consultation work?

The first meeting is free. We listen to your goals and challenges, ask questions about how your business runs today, and suggest the best next steps – with no obligation to continue.

How much do your services cost?

Every business is different, so we do not use fixed price lists. After the first consultation we send a clear quote based on the scope of work, so you know the cost before anything starts.

Can you help foreign investors set up a company?

Yes. We guide investors through company registration, licensing and the practical steps of starting to operate, and we can connect you with trusted local partners where needed.
