HR & Organisation Development

Build a strong team that grows together with your business.

As a business grows, people issues become harder to manage. Roles become unclear, hiring takes too long, good staff leave and there are no clear policies to guide decisions. These problems slow the whole business down.

We help you build the right team structure, define clear roles and put practical HR policies and processes in place, so you can attract, develop and keep the people your business needs.

What we help with

  • Organisation structure and reporting lines
  • Clear job descriptions and responsibilities
  • Hiring process and interview support
  • HR policies and an employee handbook
  • Performance reviews and staff development plans
  • Salary structure and staff retention ideas

Who is it for?

Growing businesses hiring more staff, companies without a dedicated HR team, and owners facing unclear roles, high staff turnover or team conflicts.

What you get

A clear organisation structure, documented roles and HR policies, and practical processes for hiring, reviewing and developing your people.

How we work

1
Consultation

We learn about your team, culture and the people challenges you face.

2

3

We design the structure, roles and policies that fit your business.

4
Implementation

We help introduce the changes and support managers to apply them.

Frequently asked questions

Do you replace an HR department?

No. We help you set up the structure, policies and processes. Your managers or HR staff then run them day to day, and we can provide ongoing support if needed.

Can you help us hire staff?

Yes. We can help define the role, design the hiring process and support interviews so you choose the right people.

We are a small company. Do we need HR policies?

Yes. Even simple, written policies on things like leave, working hours and conduct prevent misunderstandings and make decisions fairer and easier.

Can you help with staff performance problems?

Yes. We help you set clear expectations, introduce regular reviews and plan development, which often solves performance issues early.
