Company Setup & Market Entry

Start operating with confidence, with the right structure and paperwork from day one.

Starting a company or entering a new market involves many steps: choosing a business structure, registering with the authorities, getting the right licences and setting up basic tax and accounting. Getting these wrong can lead to delays, extra cost or problems later.

We guide you through the process step by step, explain what is required and help you prepare the documents, so you can focus on building your business instead of chasing paperwork.

What we help with

  • Choosing the right business structure for your plans
  • Company registration with the relevant authorities
  • Business licences and permits for your industry
  • Tax registration and basic compliance set-up
  • Market entry research for local and foreign investors
  • Setting up bank accounts, bookkeeping and first operating steps

Who is it for?

Entrepreneurs starting their first company, foreign investors entering a new market, and existing businesses opening a new branch or entity.

What you get

A clear checklist of what is required, help preparing and submitting documents, and guidance on staying compliant once you are operating.

Frequently asked questions

Which business structure is right for me?

It depends on factors such as ownership, the number of shareholders, your industry and future plans. We explain the options and recommend the one that fits your situation.

Can foreign investors set up a company?

In many cases, yes, although some industries have specific rules. We review your plans and explain what applies to you before you start.

How long does company registration take?

Timelines depend on the type of company, the licences required and how quickly documents are ready. We give you an estimate after the first consultation.

What happens after the company is registered?

You will need to keep up with ongoing obligations such as tax filings and licence renewals. We can help you set up the systems to stay compliant.
